# 🌳 Tree & Ensemble Algorithms Comparison Table

| Algorithm | Ensemble Type | Base Learner | Variance | Bias | Best For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Decision Tree** | Single | Tree | High | Low | Simple explainability |
| **Random Forest** | Bagging (Parallel) | Deep Trees | Low | Low | Tabular data, handles noisy data |
| **AdaBoost** | Boosting (Sequential) | Shallow Stumps | Low | Low | Clean binary classification |
| **XGBoost / LightGBM** | Gradient Boosting | Trees | Low | Low | Kaggle competitions, high precision |
